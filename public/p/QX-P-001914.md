# étale topos 分类 topos 表述

## 题面

étale topos 是何群胚的分类 topos

## 人话版

**一句话**：étale topos 是哪个局部群胚的分类 topos？

Grothendieck topos 是可以由「层」拼出来的范畴，相当于一个广义空间。分类 topos 的想法是把一个结构的所有模型装进一个 topos 里：结构的模型对应到该 topos 上的几何态射。Joyal–Tierney 定理说每个 Grothendieck topos 都是某个局部群胚（localic groupoid）的分类 topos；有足够多点的 topos 还能取拓扑群胚。题目问：对一个概型的 étale topos 这个熟悉的对象，对应的究竟是哪一个局部群胚，以及是否有人研究过。

## 原文摘录

> By a theorem of Joyal and Tierney, every Grothendieck topos is the classifying topos of a localic groupoid. It has been proved (e.g. C. Butz. and I. Moerdijk. Representing topoi by topological grupoids. Journal of Pure and Applied Algebra 130, 223-235, 1998) that topoi "with enough points" admit actually a representation as classifying topoi of topological groupoids.
> Now my question is the following: take a well-known topos, as the étale topos for a scheme. This is the classifying topos of a localic groupoid, but which one? Do you know if someone has ever investigated that? Thank you in advanc…
