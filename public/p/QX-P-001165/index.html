人话版

一句话：CAT(0) 的 free-by-cyclic 群，都虚拟特殊吗？

称 G 是 CAT(0) 群，是指它能等距、真不连续、余紧地作用在某个 CAT(0) 度量空间上。free-by-cyclic 指 G 是某个有限秩自由群被 ℤ 的扩张，即 F_m⋊ℤ 型。虚拟特殊指 G 有一个有限指数子群，同构于某个特殊复形的基本群（Haglund–Wise 意义）。右角 Artin 群（RAAG）是由一张图的顶点和交换关系给出的群。题目两问：a) 每个 CAT(0) 的 free-by-cyclic 群是否虚拟特殊？b) 较弱地问：它们是否都虚拟嵌入某个 RAAG？

原文摘录

〔21.124 〕提出人：I. Soroko
A group G is said to be virtually special if G has a finite-index subgroup isomorphic to the fundamental group of a special complex (in the sense of F. Haglund, D. T. Wise, Geom. Funct. Anal., 17, no. 5 (2008), 1551–1620; cf 20.60.) A group G is called a CAT(0) group if it acts properly discontinuously and cocompactly by isometries on a CAT(0) metric space. a) Is every CAT(0) free-by-cyclic group virtually special? b) A weaker question: does every CAT(0) free-by-cyclic group virtually embed into a right-angled Artin group? I. Soroko

—— Kourovka Notebook, Unsolved Problems in Group Theory, No. 21（E. I. Khukhro & V. D. Mazurov 编），问题 21.124（第21辑 2026 年）
