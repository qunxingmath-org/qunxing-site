# Kourovka 14.73：猜想：存在 f 使顶点传递局部-拟本原图 Γ 的价 v 处 |Γ(v)|≤f(v)

## 题面

猜想：存在 f 使顶点传递局部-拟本原图 Γ 的价 v 处 |Γ(v)|≤f(v)

### 形式化表述

∃f: |Aut(Γ)_v| ≤ f(valency) for locally-quasiprimitive vt graphs

## 人话版

**一句话**：局部拟本原的顶点传递图，给定价的顶点稳定子能有多大？

顶点传递指自同构群在顶点集上传递；Γ(v) 是 v 的邻点集，其大小就是图的价。拟本原指群作用在任何非平凡正规子群下都传递或半传递，是一种弱化的本原性。猜想是：存在只依赖价 v 的函数 f，使这类图的顶点稳定子 Aut(Γ)_v 的元素个数不超过 f(v)，即价的有限性蕴含稳定子大小的有限性。原文指出只需考虑正规子群在顶点上至多两个轨道的情形。

> Conjecture: There is a function f on the natural numbers such that, if Γ is
> a finite, vertex-transitive, locally-quasiprimitive graph of valency v, then the number
> of automorphisms fixing a given vertex is at most f(v). (By definition, a vertex-
> transitive graph Γ is locally-quasiprimitive if the stabilizer in Aut(Γ) of a vertex α is
> quasiprimitive (see 14.46) in its action on the set of vertices adjacent to α.)
> To prove the conjecture above one need only consider the case where Aut(Γ) has
> the property that every non-trivial normal subgroup has at most two orbits on vertices…
