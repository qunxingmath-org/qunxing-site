人话版

一句话：无限剩余有限群里，会不会一个无限交换子群都没有？

剩余有限群指：每个非单位元 g 都能被某个有限商群「看见」，即存在有限指数的正规子群把它排除在外。问题 a)：这样的无限群是否一定含无限交换子群？原文指出它与「是否存在非单位元、其中心化子（同它交换的全部元素构成的子群）无限」等价，并引 Shunkov 的定理（有对合、且该对合的中心化子有限的周期群几乎可解），据此可先假定群中元素都是奇数阶。副问题 b) 把「剩余有限」换成「剩余 p 群」，是可能更容易的起点。

原文摘录

〔11.56 〕提出人：A. Mann
a) Does every infinite residually finite group contain an infinite abelian sub- group? This is equivalent to the following: does every infinite residually finite group contain a non-identity element with an infinite centralizer? By a famous theorem of Shunkov a torsion group with an involution having a finite centralizer is a virtually soluble group. Therefore we may assume that in our group all elements have odd order. One should start, perhaps, with the following: b) Does every infinite residually p-group contain an infinite abelian subgroup? A. Mann

—— Kourovka Notebook, Unsolved Problems in Group Theory, No. 21（E. I. Khukhro & V. D. Mazurov 编），问题 11.56（第11辑 1990 年）
