谱隙提升到覆盖空间（MO#306102 已有断言性回答；题面校准）

人话版

一句话：底流形上有了谱隙，它的覆盖空间也一定有吗？

设 M 是完备黎曼流形。Laplace–Beltrami 算子在 M 上本质自伴，有唯一自伴扩张 Δ_M，谱落在 [0,∞) 内；记 λ(M) 为谱中非零部分的下确界。M 紧时 λ(M)>0 且就是最小非零特征值；M=Rⁿ 时 λ(M)=0。问题问：若 M 本身有谱隙 λ(M)>0，它的覆盖空间 M̃ 是否也一定有谱隙。覆盖映射把函数拉回，所以 λ(M̃)≤λ(M)，真正的疑问是会不会反而掉到 0——也就是说，底空间的谱隙能否「提升」到覆盖上。卡上注明已有断言性回答。

（AI 起草，未经人工审校）

原文摘录

Let $M$ a complete Riemannian manifold. It is well known that the Laplace-Beltrami operator on $M$ is essentially self-adjoint and thus has a unique self-adjoint extension $\Delta_M$ in $L^2(M)$. The spectrum $\sigma(\Delta_M)$ is contained in $[0,\infty)$ and we denote
$$
\lambda(M):=\inf\{\mu\in\sigma(\Delta_M)\vert \mu\neq 0\}.
$$
If $M$ is closed, then $\lambda(M)$ is positive and coincides with the smallest nonzero eigenvalue. However $\lambda(M)$ may very well be $0$, as it is the case for $M=\mathbb{R}^n$. (Non-compact examples with $\lambda(M)>0$ are provided by simply connected spaces…
