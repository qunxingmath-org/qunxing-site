人话版

一句话：把 Engel 条件放宽成「最终落入有限集」，这些元素还能凑成子群吗？

把换位子 [...[[x,g],g],…,g] 记作 g 重复 n 次的 [x, _n g]。元素 g 称几乎 Engel 的，若存在有限子集 E(g)，使对每个 x∈G，当 g 重复足够多次时该换位子总落在 E(g) 里；若能把 E(g) 取成 {1}，g 就叫 Engel 元素。Gruenberg（1966）证明了线性群中 Engel 元素全体构成子群。问题问：线性群中几乎 Engel 元素全体是否也构成子群？也就是把「恒等于 1」放宽为「最终落在有限集」之后，子群封闭性是否仍然保持。

原文摘录

提出人：P. Shumyatsky
An element g of a group G is said to be almost Engel if there is a fi-
nite subset E(g) of G such that for every x ∈G all sufficiently long commutators
[...[[x, g], g], . . . , g] belong to E(g), that is, there is a positive integer n(x, g) such that
[...[[x, g], g], . . . , g] ∈E(g) if g is repeated ⩾n(x, g) times. An element g is Engel if
we can take E(g) = {1}. The set of Engel elements of a linear group is a subgroup
by a well-known result of Gruenberg (J. Algebra, 3 (1966), 291–303). Is the set of
almost Engel elements of a linear group a subgroup?
