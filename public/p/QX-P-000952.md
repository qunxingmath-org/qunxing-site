## 人话版

**一句话**：三个子群两两相乘都得到 G，N-临界图会不会正好是三者之并？

Schmidt (p,q)-群指这样的有限群：本身不幂零，但所有真子群都幂零，且其 Sylow p-子群正规（该群必是 p 与 q 两个素数决定的）。有限群 G 的 N-临界图 Γ_Nc(G) 是这样一张有向图：顶点是 |G| 的全部素因子；(p,q) 是一条边当且仅当 G 含有 Schmidt (p,q)-子群。题目设子群 A、B、C 满足 G=AB=AC=BC（两两相乘都得到整个 G），问是否一定有 Γ_Nc(G) = Γ_Nc(A) ∪ Γ_Nc(B) ∪ Γ_Nc(C)。已知当 A、B、C 都可解时成立；反例可以取一个有限群，直接算出各张图对照即可核对。

## 原文摘录

> 提出人：V. I. Murashka, A. F. Vasil’ev
> A Schmidt (p, q)-group is a finite non-nilpotent group all of whose proper
> subgroups are nilpotent and whose Sylow p-subgroup is normal. The N-critical graph
> ΓNc(G) of a finite group G is a directed graph on the vertex set of all prime divisors of
> |G| in which (p, q) is an edge of ΓNc(G) if and only if G has a Schmidt (p, q)-subgroup.
> Suppose that a finite group G is such that G = AB = AC = BC, where A, B, C are
> subgroups of G. Is ΓNc(G) = ΓNc(A) ∪ΓNc(B) ∪ΓNc(C)? This is true if A, B, C are
> soluble.
> V. I. Murashka, A. F. Vasil’ev
>
> —— Kourovka Notebook, Unsolved Problems in Group Theory, No. 21（E. I. Khukhro & V. D. Mazurov 编），问题 20.66（第20辑 2022 年）
