Legendary 43 problems

Difficulty has four tiers: Entry, Intermediate, Advanced and Legendary. A problem is Legendary only if all three hold: it has been open for 30+ years; it is famous beyond its field (its own encyclopedia entry, a famous problem list, or a prize); and it is widely considered extremely hard. The one-line reasons are on the Chinese page.

“Check a claimed proof” means someone has claimed a proof of the conjecture and it is waiting to be checked.

This site does not verify. Problem status comes from the literature and the card maintainers’ records; it has not been checked by this site, and no submission counts as verified here.
