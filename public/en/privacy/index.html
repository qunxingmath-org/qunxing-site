Privacy Policy

Covers the website www.qunxingmath.org and the Qunxing desktop app. Last updated 25 November 2026.

In short

  • The website uses no cookies, runs no scripts, and loads no analytics, fonts or images from other sites. The server keeps access logs for 14 days.
  • The desktop app uploads nothing by default. Run records, proofs and costs stay on your computer.
  • Uploads happen only when you start them, in two steps, each with its own consent; you can skip either. Uploads are not open yet: until the intake service opens, the app’s upload buttons send nothing.
  • Uploaded data is stored in the United States. For users in mainland China this is a transfer of personal information abroad, and the app asks for your separate consent before any upload.

1. Who handles your information

The Qunxing Project, run by the maintainers of the GitHub organization qunxingmath-org.

Contact: team@qunxingmath.org. Please report security issues privately as described in SECURITY.md.

2. Visiting the website

The server keeps access logs:

What is loggedIP address, time, the requested URL (including any query string), response status, bytes sent, referring page (Referer) and browser identifier (User-Agent). No cookies and no request bodies.
WhyTo run the site, troubleshoot problems and prevent abuse.
WhereOn our server in Los Angeles, United States. The logs are not sent anywhere else.
How longRotated daily and deleted automatically after 14 days.

Cloudflare provides DNS for our domain, and DNS only: pages go straight from our server to you without passing through Cloudflare.

Email to team@qunxingmath.org is forwarded to the maintainer’s mailbox by Cloudflare’s email routing. We use it only to reply to you and handle your request.

4. Uploads: two steps, separate consent

This applies once the intake service opens. We will update this page to match before it does.

Step 1: submit the proof, light the star

  • Made public: the natural-language proof, the Lean project and local check parameters, the statement and statement-check record, and which models and tools were used. Lean code is published under Apache-2.0 and text under CC BY-SA 4.0. Once public, a proof can be withdrawn but not deleted.
  • Account: you link a Qunxing account by authorizing with GitHub. Our server uses the token GitHub gives it once, to look up your numeric user ID, then discards the token and keeps only the ID. The ID is used only for accountability and withdrawal; it is never published and never linked to your research data.
  • Not published: usage and cost, conversation transcripts, prompts, files and paths on your computer, machine details.

Step 2: upload research data

  • What is uploaded: tokens, time and cost at API prices for each step; models and tool versions, budget caps, number of searches, and the kinds and counts of interventions; the problem ID, the step you stopped at, and your self-reported “is it right / is it new”; an install ID (random, resettable in Settings), the run time (to the minute) and the channel (subscription or API).
  • Why: only for research statistics on how much compute AI spends on math problems and how well it does. Never used for advertising, never sold.
  • On our server: the install ID is stored only as a keyed hash (HMAC), never as is. Research data and proofs are stored separately.
  • Published: after anonymization, under CC0 1.0; released two weeks late, dated only to the week, with groups that are too small merged first.
  • Two optional extras, each ticked separately: a failure-path summary (anonymous, up to 1,000 characters, published under CC0); a nickname (reviewed by a person, then added to the contributor list).

Neither step uploads conversation transcripts, prompts, files or paths on your computer, machine details, passwords or API keys. If what we upload ever changes, we ask for your consent again.

6. How long we keep data

Website access logs14 days.
Uploaded research dataUntil the study ends or for 3 years, whichever comes first.
Account linkUntil you close your account. To handle withdrawals we keep only the withdrawal records we need.
After deletionRecords in the intake database are deleted at once, copies in backups disappear within 30 days, and later data releases no longer include them.
Content already publicA public proof can be withdrawn and is then marked “withdrawn”. Data versions already released, statistics already published and copies others have made can’t be recalled.

7. Your rights

  • Stop uploading: turn automatic uploads off in Settings at any time.
  • Delete: request deletion of a record in the desktop app. Before reinstalling, you can export your deletion credentials in Settings; if you lose them, email us and we will tell you how to provide the install ID you used.
  • Access, copy, correct, withdraw consent, close your account: email team@qunxingmath.org saying which data and what you want done. We check that it is you using as little information as we need.
  • You can also contact the providers above directly, through the channels in their privacy policies. If you need it, we will help find the relevant records.
  • Put only what is needed in your email. Never email deletion credentials; submit them in the desktop app.

8. Age

You must be 16 or older to upload data or submit proofs. Browsing the website and using the desktop app on your own computer have no age limit.

9. Security

  • The website and the intake service use HTTPS only. The intake service keeps no access logs.
  • Backups and research snapshots are encrypted before storage; the decryption keys are kept offline.
  • If we suffer or suspect a breach, we close the affected service, revoke and replace keys, and notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update this page and the date above. The full history is public on GitHub: qunxing-site. If what we upload or who receives it changes, the desktop app asks for your consent again.
